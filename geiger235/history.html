RAD AGE BEGINS

In the 1900s to 1930s radioactivity became associated with medical treatments, used for scientific progress, glowing consumer products.

People supposed health benefits, but this mistake caused deaths and illnesses like the radium girls, who used it in their makeup or were exposed to copious amounts in military glow-in-the-dark clock factories.

These flaws exposed radiation dangers changing public attitudes. Radiation was made clear not to be screwed with.

I DON’T WANT TO SET THE WORLD ON FIRE

Post 1940s, the 50s to 1990s saw a terrible Cold War between the USSR and the USA. Nuclear testing skyrocketed especially in places like Bikini Atoll and Nevada, going from dropper bombs to missiles and precise ICBMs.

The nuclear arms race between the two global superpowers saw the ability to wipe out life on earth as a simple means of pushing a button.

Civil defense, radiation based power plants, radioactive medicine, and major nuclear accidents marked the rapid expansion of technology from the Cold War drive to innovate.

Even without mutually assured destruction, modern events like Chernobyl nuclear detonation and Fukushima power plant failure saw how devastating the misshandling of such a powerful energy source could be.

RADIATION

Geiger World is an indie-online effort to document and exposit the wonders of Radiation, as a scientific phenomenon and element of our culture.

Radiation and radioactive technology is often mistaken by the layman's world as dangerous and to be feeared. This website is here to dispell those worries, and reframe the perception of this wonderful scientific miracle.

It’s a beautiful creation of God. Given the right use, it can manifest productive results, yet due to pollution and violence, it’s got a bad wrap.

WHAT IS IT?

Radiation is: 

“Radiation is energy that moves from one place to another in a form that can be described as waves or particles.”

- International Atomic Energy Agency (2023).
Radiation has been in invisible force we interact with everyday with in the sun, our consumer products, water, and medicine. 

USEFUL?

Radiation can be used in:

Health:
Bio-med procedures use radiation to diagnose cancer and image body parts for physical and genetic damage.

Energy:
It’s not just uranium power plants, but also the solar radiation that fuels our solar panels for green sustainable futures.

Protecting/Managing Nature:
Radiation is used to purify wastewater and mutate crops to be more efficient in a worsening climate.

Industrialism:
Nuclear tech uses radiation to date historical relics, strengthen chemical products, and improve parts for manufacturing. 

BOTTOM LINE...

Ionizing radiations are what we typically think of. They are the powerful portions of the electromagnetic spectrum or subatomic particles that can move with high energy to jostle around atomic structures.
